# Canvas overview

Vectary Canvas is an infinite canvas where you collect references, generate images, video and 3D with AI presets, and turn ideas into concepts, all in one place.



{% hint style="info" icon="rectangle-beta" %}
Vectary Canvas is currently in **Beta**. We're actively improving it, and some features may change.
{% endhint %}



### What you can do with Canvas



* **Collect and organize** - bring images, 3D models, PDFs, links, and Studio scenes onto one canvas and arrange them freely.<br>
* **Generate with presets** - apply a preset to your content to generate new images or 3D models. You don't pick an AI model or write a prompt: each preset is already tuned for a specific task.



* **Work with 3D directly on the canvas** - import a 3D model, open it in full-screen mode to adjust the transformation and edit materials and textures, or create a **Material** element on the canvas and apply it to a model or to a specific part of it.&#x20;



* **Collaborate** - leave comments, annotations, and sticky notes, and work on the same canvas with your team.



### How Canvas works



Every Canvas project is a single infinite canvas. Work on it follows a simple loop: **bring in content, apply a preset, and continue with the result.**



#### 1. Bring in your content



Most work starts with something you already have. Import an image, a 3D model, a PDF, or a link, or add a Studio scene, either by pasting its link or by choosing it from your library.&#x20;



#### 2. Apply a preset



A preset is a ready-made AI tool for one specific job, such as creating a photorealistic render from a sketch, extracting materials from a photo, or generating a 3D model from an image. Presets are designed to work with the content you've brought in, so you start from your own reference rather than from a blank page.

You don't choose an AI model or write a prompt. Each preset is crafted by Vectary for its task.



#### 3. Continue with the result



The result of a generation (an image, a video, or a 3D model) appears on the canvas as a new element. You can apply another preset to it, compare it with other variations, or keep refining.&#x20;





### Canvas elements



Everything on the canvas is an element. Besides imported content and generation results, you can add elements from the **+** menu to organize and annotate your work: **Prompt**, **Text**, **Frame**, **Material**, **Gallery**, **Sticky Note**, and **Annotation**.&#x20;





### Presets



A preset is a ready-made AI tool for one specific job, for example creating a photorealistic render, changing a material, or generating a 3D model from an image. Presets are designed and tested by Vectary, so you get consistent results without adjusting settings.





### A typical workflow



1. **Research** - collect references, sketches, photos, documents, and 3D models
2. **Explore** - generate and compare forms, finishes, and variations with presets
3. **Refine** - develop the strongest idea into a 3D concept: geometry, materials, lighting
4. **Validate** - compare options and gather team feedback
